# Business Analytics – Surfing Industry Analysis

## Project Overview
This project explores the surfing industry through data analysis, focusing on surfer demographics, surfing patterns, and factors influencing overall surfing performance. The objective was to identify meaningful trends, compare male and female surfing profiles, and understand the key factors associated with better surfing performance.

## Key Areas of Analysis

**1. Exploratory Data Analysis & Surfer Personas**
- Developed male and female surfer personas based on wave height, surfing frequency, speed, and overall performance.
- Compared surfing patterns and performance differences across genders.
- Analysed surfer age distribution and wave-height trends using visualisations.

**2. Performance & Correlation Analysis**
- Examined the relationship between overall surfing performance and three key variables: surfer weight, surfing frequency, and surfing speed.
- Identified performance trends, including the association between regular practice, speed ratings, and overall performance.
- Interpreted the physical and behavioural factors influencing surfing performance.

**3. Data Cleaning & Missing Values**
- Explored strategies for handling missing data, including deletion, mean, median, and mode imputation.
- Considered data distribution, variable types, and dataset integrity when selecting appropriate approaches.

**4. Business Analytics & Leadership**
- Examined the benefits of analytics-driven organisations and effective analytics leadership.
- Compared centralised and decentralised analytics structures.
- Discussed methods of assessing organisational analytics capabilities, including AI, machine learning, and real-time data processing.

**5. Predictive Analytics – Real-World Case Study**
- Explored Ford Motor Company's application of predictive analytics to improve carpet quality in its 2011 Ford Fiesta.
- Examined the use of Minitab and Design of Experiments (DOE) to identify optimal manufacturing settings and resolve quality issues.

## Key Takeaways
The analysis highlighted how data visualisation and statistical comparisons can reveal meaningful patterns in surfing behaviour and performance. It also demonstrated how predictive analytics can support business decision-making, improve operational efficiency, and address real-world business challenges.
